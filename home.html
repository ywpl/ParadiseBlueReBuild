Welcome Aboard Paradise Blue — a modern pontoon boat designed and handcrafted in Bayswater, Western Australia. Combining innovation, comfort, and sustainability, Paradise Blue is more than just a boat — it's your floating escape.

Built for coastal cruising, river relaxation, and social adventures, Paradise Blue features a spacious layout, luxurious lounge seating, and cutting-edge technology powered by solar-regenerated batteries. It’s the perfect blend of style and performance. Proudly Australian design. Boldly redefining the future of pontoon boats.

Step into a new wave of leisure boating with Paradise Blue. We craft comfort, technology, and serenity so every voyage feels like drifting through calm ocean water.
